# Ontario posting observations

Use only when relevant to a requested Ontario role. Checked October 25, 2026; recheck current official sources when a decision depends on them. These are observations, not findings that an employer broke the law.

First establish ESA coverage, posting scope and the 25-employee threshold at posting. Internal postings, general campaigns and work outside the Act's scope can be excluded. Federal labour jurisdiction is separate; an Ontario worksite alone does not establish ESA coverage. See the [regulation](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/regulation/240476) and [coverage guidance](https://www.ontario.ca/document/employment-standard-act-policy-and-interpretation-manual/part-iii-how-act-applies).

For covered public postings, note expected compensation, AI use in screening/assessment/selection, vacancy disclosure and Canadian-experience requirements. The annualized compensation range spread is capped at C$50,000. Disclosure exceptions apply when expected annual compensation, or the range's upper end, exceeds $200,000. A posted range does not guarantee a particular offer. Preserve unknowns and exact wording. See [official posting guidance](https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/requirements-related-publicly-advertised-job).

The notification obligation concerns whether a hiring decision has been made, not a promise of a job or a personalized explanation. Preliminary screening is excluded from the interview definition. Normally notice is due within 45 days after the last interview; the guide specifies notice after each interview when rounds are more than 45 days apart. Borderline timing needs source review. A portal update can qualify when the applicant receives a link. Check the actual event before deriving a follow-up date; do not start a statutory clock for every recruiter message.

Link the relevant rule and record unresolved coverage rather than accusing the employer. A possible omission can inform a factual question; it does not establish a fake vacancy, guaranteed pay or permission to contact anyone.
