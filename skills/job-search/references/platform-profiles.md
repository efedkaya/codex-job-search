# Audit and update candidate profiles

Use this workflow for LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor and Canada Job Bank. A profile audit is read-only; a request to update the named profiles authorizes ordinary evidence-backed edits and relevant résumé replacement within that scope. It does not authorize applications, outreach, new credentials, demographic answers, or changes to privacy, visibility, notifications or linked-account permissions. Follow the host's confirmation rules for any separate consequential action.

## Compare before editing

Bind the user's actual open tabs and verify account identity. Reuse the designated private profile, current résumé, portfolio and work evidence under [profile](profile.md). Preserve a compact private before/after record of affected professional fields and uploaded file names, excluding unrelated account content and sensitive identifiers.

Review the fields each site actually exposes: name and professional links, headline/about, employers/titles/dates, current versus completed education, supported skills, résumé, target titles, desired worksites/remote scope, employment types, pay floor and availability. Resolve material factual conflicts before saving. Retain correct content; do not rewrite every section just to register an edit. Use [applications](applications.md) for résumé checks.

Actual residence, desired job locations and willingness to relocate are different fields. Do not move the candidate's home location to a target worksite, claim relocation to unlock a location control, translate a minimum salary into a desired salary, or turn preferred job seniority into years of experience. A platform's title/NOC options may be coarse: select a defensible match, record that limitation and preserve the broader search plan privately. Do not enter a government identifier, infer employment-group membership or upgrade account access just to complete a profile.

## Save and verify

Prepare wording from supported claims; keep assistance, shared employment and in-progress credentials accurate. Avoid employer/client confidential details and unsupported proficiency. Check résumé autofill before accepting it, and preserve the previous local original and the exact new upload.

Check visible sync notices before editing: Indeed and Glassdoor may share contact data, preferences or a résumé. Edit shared fields once, then inspect the other site; report confirmed propagation separately from unverified sync. Do not remove and recreate a résumé when a replacement control exists. If replacement has no recoverable original, preserve/download it first where possible; follow host rules before irreversible deletion.

After saving, inspect the persisted values by returning to the relevant section or reopening its editor. A click or loading state is not a verified save. Capture useful screenshot proof under the host's browser rules. Keep each site outcome separate: updated and verified, reviewed with no change needed, partially updated, or blocked with the exact missing fact/control/access. Do not claim a profile update refreshed a job search, changed recommendations successfully, or submitted applications.
