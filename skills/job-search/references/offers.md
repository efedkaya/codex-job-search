# Compare offers and prepare negotiation

Use for a requested offer comparison, compensation discussion or reading of supplied employment terms. Start with the actual offer and the candidate's stated priorities, current constraints and relevant application/interview notes. Work from available material without requiring a full contract for a simple comparison. Keep employer terms, candidate preferences and market context distinct.

## Compare the same things

Build a compact table only for material terms. Preserve the original document/date and label information as advertised, discussed, written offer or signed terms. A newer verbal claim does not silently replace a written term; show the discrepancy and the question needed to resolve it.

- Employer entity, role/scope, location, attendance, start date, hours and employment type.
- Guaranteed base pay, currency and pay period; bonus/commission amount, conditions and payout timing.
- Benefits, leave, probation, notice, signing/relocation payments and repayment conditions.
- Equity instrument/amount, vesting and exercise terms when relevant; keep uncertain equity value separate from guaranteed cash.
- Candidate priorities such as development, support, commute, flexibility and financial needs, using their own weighting if a ranking is requested.

Convert units only with explicit assumptions, such as paid hours/weeks; do not compare hourly gross pay with annual take-home pay. Keep target variable compensation separate from guaranteed earnings. Missing terms stay unknown rather than zero or “standard.” A missing referenced plan or policy limits the review; identify it instead of inventing its content. Recheck current market data, exchange rates or other changeable comparisons when needed, with direct sources and dates.

## Read terms against what was promised

For a document review, cite the actual clause/section and explain its plain-language effect, including omissions that matter to a recorded promise. Check relevant terms such as variable-pay discretion, outside-work/IP scope, restrictions after departure, termination, contingencies, dispute terms and repayment. Do not label a clause fair, lawful, enforceable or normal merely because it is familiar.

Separate questions the employer can answer from jurisdiction-dependent legal questions. If a legal or tax conclusion is requested, establish the applicable jurisdiction and verify current authoritative sources; explain the limits and identify material questions for a qualified adviser. Do not impose a mandatory legal consultation or signing hold on an ordinary comparison. Descriptive review does not establish legal compliance, future equity value or immigration eligibility.

## A negotiation draft grounded in real leverage

Use the candidate's requested outcome, priorities, supported contribution and relevant verified market context. Offer a specific ask or options when the facts support them; explain the tradeoff between cash, flexibility, start date or other terms rather than applying a universal percentage uplift.

Do not invent a competing offer, deadline, existing salary or willingness to walk away. A desired amount and a number already communicated to the employer are different facts. Ask only for a missing decision that materially changes the draft. Preserve previously communicated terms and flag a change of position for review.

Deliver a comparison and/or requested reply draft with unresolved points. Store versions with the relevant private application attempt when persistence is useful; neither a draft nor offer_received is acceptance. A signed offer, actual acceptance, message delivery or withdrawal from another process requires its own supported event and the current action authorization.
