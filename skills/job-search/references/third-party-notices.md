| Upstream material | Adaptation |
|---|---|
| `modes/oferta.md` | Separate posting importance from candidate evidence; keep consequential gaps visible; describe posting concerns as observations |

| `modes/interview/plan.md`, `practice.md`, `debrief.md` | Bounded preparation, reusable situation/task/action/result/reflection stories, one-question coaching and source-labelled debriefs |
