# Shared employer priorities

The [bundled seed data](priority-employers.json) contains 241 distinct employers from a shared global-company list and a shared Canadian list: 173 + 74 rows with 6 overlaps. It includes major technology companies, the Big Four, banks, insurers, retailers, industrial employers and private/Crown organizations. It contains names and supplied category labels, not candidate information or current vacancies.

## Use as a default priority, not an eligibility shortcut

Prioritize these employers for the current candidate unless they change or decline that preference. Briefly explain the default during setup; do not require them to inspect all 241 names. They can prioritize sectors, select names, add companies or exclude employers. Preserve separately named entities such as Brookfield Corporation and Brookfield Asset Management; a list overlap is not permission to collapse distinct hiring companies.

Once background, location and the necessary hard limits are known, choose a bounded, relevant first pass. Search plausible functions and official regional hiring entities instead of launching 241 complete inventories at first use. The candidate may have a different occupation, country, pay floor or seniority than other users. A company in the seed list may have no suitable work in their area. Continue useful non-list discovery alongside it, especially when another employer offers a better supported match.

Use the [search-angle register and coverage rules](search-angles.md#make-a-company-pass-auditable). Copy needed names into that person's private target register with pending status; the bundled data has no inherited searched/verified state. Each employer's checks need current sources, actual scope and honest completion/partial/blocker reporting. No hits from an index do not prove no vacancies. A request for all companies or all openings requires explicit scope and actual traversal; a default priority does not imply those inventories have already been reviewed.

Verify current corporate identity, local legal employer and official careers/ATS routes before relying on a posting. A reseller, staffing client or partner is not automatically employment by the famous brand. Category labels and the original lists are not independently verified index-membership, market-cap, company-size or workplace-quality claims. No ticker or investing analysis is needed for a job search.

Apply this candidate's actual location/remote eligibility, guaranteed pay, required experience, credentials and task evidence before ranking. Show realistic stretches and unknowns separately from established hard mismatches. Brand preference cannot override a hard condition or establish hiring probability. Applications require the candidate to choose the vacancy and retain their final-review mode.
