# Writing application documents

Use [applications](applications.md) for evidence selection, factual review, requested editing scope and versions. Use [document quality](document-quality.md) for the final exported artifact and optional checker. This guide develops supported content; it does not introduce a second evidence ledger or mandatory questionnaire.

## Find the useful contribution

For a vague duty, find one actual episode: what needed attention, what the candidate personally did, what changed and how they know. Ask a focused question only when the answer would improve the requested work. A responsible recurring duty, concrete deliverable or qualitative improvement can be useful evidence without an achievement number.

Choose a form that fits the evidence:

| Evidence | Useful form |
|---|---|
| Supported result and a defensible connection to the action | Result, context and personal method |
| A difficult situation or decision | Brief challenge, action and supported result/reflection |
| Responsibility or scale without outcome data | Action, object and actual scope |
| Observed improvement without a useful measure | Concrete change and who benefited |

Keep exact figures, candidate estimates, document facts, calculations and unknowns distinguishable through existing evidence pointers. Preserve the base, time period, units and personal/team attribution. Show a derived calculation and its assumptions; clarify an uncertain premise rather than asking the candidate to approve routine arithmetic. Never suggest a number for them to confirm. If causation is uncertain, describe the action and observed result without claiming the action caused it.

An action verb must preserve the contribution: assisted work stays assisted. Prefer concrete wording over inflated verbs and vague scale words. Vary phrasing when it improves readability, with no verb-use quota, required proportion of numeric bullets or universal word count. Retain enough context for the candidate to explain the claim honestly.

## Tailor with proportionate changes

Use the smallest edit that fulfills the request: critique, a focused passage, light emphasis changes or a substantial restructure. Reuse an explicit standing preference; resolve an ambiguous consequential choice without forcing an intensity-selection step for ordinary edits. Choose complementary examples before polishing sentences and preserve employment chronology and fact associations.

Make the target contribution clear near the beginning with the strongest relevant proof. Use the posting's terminology only when the work actually supports it. A term can be absent from a draft yet supported elsewhere; inspect that evidence or ask where/how the candidate used it. Do not add the term to Skills and several bullets mechanically, force adjacent work into an identical capability, or optimize a repetition count.

Review changed claims for interview defensibility: what does each claim mean, what did the candidate do and how is the result known? Clarify the particular doubtful claim; a universal approval question before every draft is unnecessary. Keep unresolved placeholders clearly inside drafts and remove or resolve them before a finished deliverable.

## Letters and form answers

A letter should connect the actual role, relevant employer context and one or two supported examples. Use the requested length or employer limit; otherwise keep the detail proportionate to what the letter adds. A proposed approach can explain a transition when labelled as a proposal. Keep genuine candidate motivation, use a neutral greeting when no recipient is verified and avoid invented enthusiasm or company facts. A focused edit preserves unaffected text and the latest draft.

Read each form question and its actual field type. A project entry uses the project's scope, dates and personal role; it does not automatically inherit all employment tenure. For years-of-experience questions, count relevant supported periods without double-counting overlap or converting assistance into independent experience; state approximations when allowed.

A numeric salary field needs a candidate-chosen amount in its currency and period. Research can inform that choice; it is not the candidate's expectation. Do not paste a range into a single-number field or use placeholder tricks to advance. Count actual final text against meaningful limits with the available tools or [material checker](document-quality.md#optional-offline-mechanical-helper), naming any unavailable check.

Follow employer requirements and relevant local conventions. Page counts, column layouts and common résumé advice are defaults to assess against the actual document, not universal screening facts. Do not repeat unsourced rejection statistics, invented ATS scores or promises that a writing formula increases interview odds.
